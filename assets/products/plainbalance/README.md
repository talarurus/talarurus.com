# plainbalance website assets

The unchanged outlined `lockup.svg` is the approved lowercase plainbalance
wordmark with secondary `by Talarurus` attribution. Keep its width at least
200px. `app-icon.svg` is an unchanged copy of the approved original white
stepped-corner on its black rounded-square tile, from
`brand/plainbalance/placeholder/app-icon.svg` at the same reviewed source commit.
Both are product assets, separate from the official Talarurus corporate
logo and site favicon, whose existing rights and geometry are unchanged.

The four PNGs in `v0.3.1/` are unedited browser captures of the working shared
frontend with a Rust storage test harness and the project's canonical synthetic
ledger. They show fictional financial records, not personal information. They
are not native desktop screenshots, installed-app certification or evidence of
a consumer release. The selected views are Overview (light), Spending (dark),
Cash flow (light) and Accounts (light). Their local capture evidence was reviewed
on 2026-10-11. Future captures should retain a version, runtime and data label.

Product artwork and the original application UI source are MPL-2.0; the full
license is in `LICENSE.txt`. The outlined lockup originates from
`brand/plainbalance/lockup.svg` at reviewed source commit
`b6fcd4c94ba4ca75d56dd2a3ecfcf93257d4873a` in
https://github.com/talarurus/plainbalance. Original product assets, source
notices and separate font/publisher terms remain available there. Inter source
fonts are OFL-1.1, with notices in the project's
`brand/plainbalance/source/fonts/OFL.txt`; no font file is included in this asset
directory. Existing website fonts retain their own `assets/fonts/OFL.txt` notice.
No trademark registration or legal clearance is implied.

These files are prepared for founder review. Their presence locally does not
authorize website deployment or separate media publication.
