Release roadmap ↗

Local-first, with limits

Your records.
Still experimental.

No cloud account or bank connection is required. Records stay in a local profile.

Use fictional financial records only. Local databases and exported backups are unencrypted. Amount masking and checksums do not provide encryption.

Consumer installers are withheld until encryption, protected backups and recovery are reviewed. The preview is not ready for sensitive records.
